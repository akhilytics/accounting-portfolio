## 1. Accounts Receivable Aging & Collections Prioritization
`AR_Aging_Analysis.xlsx`

A formula-driven AR aging model over a 1,750-invoice sample dataset.

- Buckets open invoices by overdue period (Not Due, 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days) using COUNTIF / SUMIF logic
- Ranks customers by outstanding balance to prioritize collections by exposure
- Computes total outstanding, overdue amount, critical-risk balances and a recommended provision (% of receivables)
- Summary tab auto-calculates from the raw data — change the data and KPIs update

**Skills:** Accounts Receivable (O2C), AR aging, collections analysis, Advanced Excel (COUNTIF, SUMIF, SUMIFS, pivot tables), provisioning, MIS reporting

## 2. Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Grade-Wise Pricing & Cost Model
`RMC_Pricing_Model.xlsx`

A costing model that derives grade-wise selling prices (M10–M25) from first principles.

- Builds raw-material cost per CBM for each grade from mix ratios and input rates
- Separates variable costs (diesel, transport, handling) from fixed costs (salaries, rent, EMIs, plant)
- Allocates monthly fixed cost per CBM by expected volume, then applies a target margin to set price
- Includes a pump-cost recovery sub-model and break-even volume logic

**Skills:** Management / cost accounting, cost-volume-profit, pricing strategy, contribution & break-even analysis, Advanced Excel modeling, variance analysis
